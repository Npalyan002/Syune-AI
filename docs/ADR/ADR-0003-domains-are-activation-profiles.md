# ADR-0003 — Domains Are Activation Profiles

**Status:** Accepted  
**Baseline:** v001 / Baseline 0.1

## Decision

Creative, finance, systems, and strategy are activation/reasoning profiles over shared memory.

## Rationale

One item may matter in multiple contexts.

## Consequences and boundaries

Profiles cannot own exclusive facts or hide provenance.

See [Constitution](../SYUNE_CONSTITUTION_v001.md) and [Blueprint](../SYUNE_BLUEPRINT_v001.md).
