# ADR-0008 — Step 01 30 Functional Not Topological

**Status:** Accepted  
**Baseline:** v001 / Baseline 0.1

## Decision

STEP 01–30 are functional cognitive requirements mapped across capabilities, not folders/services/agents.

## Rationale

Research steps describe functions rather than deployment boundaries.

## Consequences and boundaries

No step01–step30 runtime topology is created.

See [Constitution](../SYUNE_CONSTITUTION_v001.md) and [Blueprint](../SYUNE_BLUEPRINT_v001.md).
