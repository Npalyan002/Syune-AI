# Lifecycle security

Lifecycle never widens `SecurityEnvelope`. Retrieval authorization runs after lifecycle eligibility and also applies to explicit archive access. Exact-duplicate identity includes fact/context identity and consolidation does not synthesize a broader-security record. Callers must not consolidate across incompatible principals, scopes, sensitivities, or purposes; uncertain matches remain separate.

Archived and forgotten records are absent from derived lexical/vector indexes. Graph expansion rejects non-active neighbors. Invalidated evidence is archived and is unavailable to ordinary learning/cognition.
