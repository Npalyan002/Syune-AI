# Evidence independence

Evidence is clustered by overlapping provenance lineage, source document, external event, replay, task instance, and self-evaluator identity. Transitive overlaps merge clusters. Each cluster contributes only its strongest positive and strongest negative quality score.

Verification requires two independent positive clusters and weighted support of 1.4 by default. Negative quality is preserved and can dispute, demote, or revoke. Diversity is a signal, not a truth guarantee. The adversarial test demonstrates that 100 duplicated agents remain one cluster and do not mechanically outvote an independent high-quality failure.
