# Knowledge utility

Six organizational procedures were promoted. Across 80 held-out tasks, promoted knowledge was retrieved and used 60 times: 60 helpful, zero neutral, zero harmful. Utility precision was 100%. Twenty tasks had no applicable organizational procedure, producing 25% learned-knowledge abstention.

Four local procedures and six organizational procedures were produced from eight hypotheses. Growth plateaus after 100 synthetic experiences: repeated evidence does not create new procedures. At 1,000 and 10,000 checkpoints, one procedure is archived while nine remain active. The shape is healthy for this fixture, but the small eight-family ontology also makes saturation easy; broader production diversity is required.
