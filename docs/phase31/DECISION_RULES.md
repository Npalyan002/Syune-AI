# Frozen product-decision rules

Rules were serialized in `preregistration.json` before live treatment. Full product requires
all security/truth gates, quality noninferiority, at least four multi-dimensional wins, and a
complexity component ratio no greater than two. Lean product requires all hard gates,
quality noninferiority and at least two learning/continuity/economic wins. A governed-memory
pivot requires comparable quality with insufficient learning/economic/continuity advantage
and complete governed-core audit. Otherwise the classifier returns
`DO_NOT_PRODUCTIZE_CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE`.

The live outcome was not manually selected. The truth hard gate failed, quality
noninferiority was not established, and the complexity ratio was 3.0.
