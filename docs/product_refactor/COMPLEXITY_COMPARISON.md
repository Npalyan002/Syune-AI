# Complexity comparison

The default runtime drops from 13 dataclass-held components (including four research/learning services and four repositories) to 9 core fields plus optional `None` slots, with two repositories open instead of four. Health components fall from 10 mixed product/research checks to 8 core/availability checks. Research construction sites on startup fall from four to zero.

Public product concepts are now eight core verbs instead of a cognition/council/planner ladder. Source compatibility costs three deprecated lazy methods. Schema-v1 still creates two legacy database artifacts; removal is deferred to a state-schema migration to avoid destructive upgrades.
